# Parking

## General rules
Parking is free for patients for up to 3 hours with validation at the front desk. After that, the rate is 10 reais per hour.

## Gate
The entrance gate opens automatically for registered residents and staff. Registered users can ask for it to be opened on this call by giving their plate.

## Hours
The parking lot is open 24 hours for residents and from 7 am to 8 pm for visitors.
